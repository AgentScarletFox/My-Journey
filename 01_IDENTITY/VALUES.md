# 💎 VALUES

> The things that matter most to me.

**Last Updated:** 2026-08-11

---

# 🌟 My Core Values

## 01 — Growth

**Why it matters:**

I want to keep learning, improving, and becoming better than I was before.

I don't want to stop growing just because I reach one goal.

---

## 02 — Curiosity

**Why it matters:**

I want to understand the world instead of simply accepting things without questioning them.

Curiosity pushes me to explore science, technology, research, ideas, and new experiences.

---

## 03 — Integrity

**Why it matters:**

I want my actions to match my words.

I want to be honest with myself and others, even when doing the right thing is difficult.

---

## 04 — Independence

**Why it matters:**

I want to develop the ability to think for myself, make informed decisions, take responsibility, and create my own path.

---

## 05 — Contribution

**Why it matters:**

I don't want success to be only about myself.

I want what I learn and build to eventually create value for other people and contribute something meaningful.

---

# 🌱 How I Want to Live

I want to:

* Stay curious.
* Keep learning.
* Work hard without losing myself.
* Build instead of only consuming.
* Treat people with respect.
* Take responsibility for my choices.
* Stay open to changing my mind.
* Make time for both ambition and life.
* Keep exploring different possibilities.
* Become someone I genuinely respect.

---

# 🚫 What I Will Not Compromise

I will not compromise:

* My integrity for success.
* My values for approval.
* My long-term growth for short-term comfort.
* My health for unnecessary achievement.
* Respect for other people.
* My curiosity because something is difficult.
* My identity just to fit someone else's expectations.

---

# 🎯 My Definition of Success

Success is not just:

* Getting the highest rank.
* Getting into a prestigious university.
* Winning competitions.
* Building successful projects.
* Earning money.
* Collecting achievements.

Those things can be valuable, but they are **not the entire definition of success**.

For me, success means:

> **Becoming a capable, curious, independent, and kind person while building a life that I am proud of.**

I want to learn deeply, create meaningful things, pursue ambitious goals, maintain strong relationships, take care of myself, and contribute to others.

If I can look back and say:

**"I used my opportunities well, kept learning, stayed true to myself, and helped make something better,"**

then I will consider that success.

---

> **My goals may change. My interests may change. My plans may change.**
>
> **My values are the foundation that helps me navigate those changes.**
