# 🔧 IMPROVEMENTS

> Areas I want to improve without judging myself.

**Last Updated:** 2026-08-11

---

## Areas I Need to Improve

### 1. Trying to Do Too Many Things

**Problem:**
I have many interests and often want to work on academics, programming, AI, projects, competitions, research, reading, activities, and personal goals at the same time.

**Why it happens:**
I'm curious about many different areas and don't want to miss opportunities.

**What I can do:**
Choose a small number of priorities and protect time for them. Everything else can wait.

---

### 2. Consistency

**Problem:**
Starting something is easier for me than maintaining it consistently over a long period.

**Why it happens:**
Motivation changes, and I sometimes focus too much on the result instead of the process.

**What I can do:**
Focus on showing up regularly, even on days when progress is small.

---

### 3. Overthinking & Planning

**Problem:**
I can spend too much time planning, organizing, researching, or trying to make something perfect before actually doing it.

**Why it happens:**
I want to make the right decision and avoid wasting time.

**What I can do:**
Set a reasonable plan, start quickly, learn from the result, and improve along the way.

---

## Patterns I Notice

* I sometimes take on more than I can realistically manage.
* I can prioritize planning over execution.
* I sometimes want fast progress.
* I need to become better at focusing on one thing at a time.
* Consistency matters more than occasional bursts of motivation.

---

## Things I Am Working On

* [ ] Prioritize before starting new things
* [ ] Protect my study time
* [ ] Finish what I start
* [ ] Reduce unnecessary planning
* [ ] Take action before everything is perfect
* [ ] Review my progress regularly
* [ ] Focus on long-term improvement

---

## Progress

**Current status:** 🟡 Working on it

I don't expect these weaknesses to disappear overnight.

The goal is to become a little better at managing them every day.
