# 🧭 PRINCIPLES

> Rules I choose to guide my decisions and actions.

**Last Updated:** 2026-08-11

---

## My Principles

### 01 — Consistency Over Perfection

I don't need to be perfect every day.

I need to keep moving forward.

---

### 02 — Protect What Matters Most

My biggest goals deserve my best time and attention.

I will prioritize what matters instead of trying to do everything at once.

---

### 03 — Learn, Then Apply

I don't want to only consume information.

Whenever possible, I will practice, build, experiment, and apply what I learn.

---

### 04 — Be Curious

I will ask questions, explore new ideas, and stay open to changing my mind when I discover something better.

---

### 05 — Learn From Failure

A bad result is information.

I will identify what went wrong, learn from it, and try again instead of giving up.

---

### 06 — Build My Own Identity

I will not measure my worth only through grades, rankings, awards, or other people's opinions.

I want to become someone I respect.

---

### 07 — Think Long Term

I will make decisions based not only on what feels good today, but also on the person I want to become years from now.

---

# 🧠 When Making Decisions

I ask:

* Does this align with my goals?
* Does this respect my values?
* Will this help me grow?
* Is this worth my time?
* Am I doing this because I genuinely want to, or because of pressure?
* Will this help or distract me from my priorities?
* Will I be proud of this decision later?

---

# ⚠️ When Things Go Wrong

I will:

1. Pause instead of reacting emotionally.
2. Understand what happened.
3. Accept what I can control.
4. Identify what I can learn.
5. Fix what I can.
6. Try again.
7. Move forward without constantly replaying the mistake.

> **Failure is feedback, not identity.**

---

# 🏆 When I Succeed

I will:

* Be grateful.
* Enjoy the achievement.
* Give credit to the people who helped me.
* Record what I learned.
* Avoid becoming complacent.
* Set the next meaningful challenge.

> **Celebrate progress without stopping growth.**

---

# 🌱 The Person I Want to Be

I want to become someone who is:

* Curious
* Disciplined
* Kind
* Independent
* Resilient
* Honest
* Responsible
* Confident without being arrogant
* Willing to learn
* Willing to help others
* Comfortable with failure
* Capable of solving difficult problems
* Consistent with the things that matter

I want my identity to be built through **what I repeatedly do**, not only what I say I want to become.

---

> **My principles are a compass, not a cage.**
>
> They can evolve as I learn and grow, but they should always help me make better decisions.
